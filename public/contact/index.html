Privacy requests and reports

Use privacy@ogcupid.com to ask about personal data or request assistance with deletion. Signed-in members can also request account deletion through their account controls, subject to reauthentication.

Use security@ogcupid.com to report a vulnerability or suspected unauthorized access. Please don't send credentials, passwords, private keys, recovery codes, or other people's sensitive information. Start with a short description and a safe reproduction outline.

For urgent threats to anyone's immediate safety, contact local emergency services. These inboxes are not monitored as an emergency response line.

Before you email

OG Cupid is under active construction. Email is handled manually, and no specific response-time guarantee is offered. The availability of an email address doesn't mean every requested feature or service is already live.
