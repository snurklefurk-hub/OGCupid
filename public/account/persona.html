How were my scores calculated?

Statements and situations explore four classic dimensions. Each scored answer contributes only to the dimension it describes; balanced or not-applicable answers contribute zero. Three historical interludes are just for fun. Scores choose a leaning, not an absolute identity. A tied score uses the majority of directional answers, then the earliest strongest answer; a dimension with no directional evidence defaults to the first trait. The original personas are retained, but these are revival questions and scoring, not the recovered original algorithm. The descriptions are comic archetypes, not diagnoses or measured predictions about your partners.
