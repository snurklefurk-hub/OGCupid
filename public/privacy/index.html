1. Who operates OG Cupid and what this policy covers

OG Cupid ("OG Cupid," "we," "us" or "our") is an independently operated dating-site project. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information through the OG Cupid website, member accounts and other features that link to this notice. For privacy inquiries, use privacy@ogcupid.com. Other contacts are listed on our Contact page.

OG Cupid is under active construction. The statements below describe the features currently in use, not every future dating, media, matching or paid feature discussed on the blog. Links to external resources and third-party sites have their own privacy practices and are not covered by this notice.

2. Categories of information we handle

Information you give us

  • Membership information: your registration email address, name or display name, age confirmation, and information needed to verify or recover your account. A managed authentication provider processes passwords and related authentication data. We do not store readable passwords in our private member-profile table.
  • Private profile information: what you choose to enter about yourself—biography, essays, age, town or city, gender, pronouns, orientation, relationship goals, preferences and lifestyle details. Some answers can reveal sensitive personal characteristics, beliefs or intimate preferences. You decide which optional fields to fill.
  • Answers and settings: match-question selections, answers you would accept from others, importance ratings, any optional notes, answer-privacy selections, and other member controls.
  • Location and radius preferences: a town or city selected from the site's location index and, optionally, a search origin and mileage radius. We store the location index identifier and approximate town-center coordinates for those selections—not your precise residence or continuous GPS location.
  • Optional email categories: preferences for account activity, messages, site news or necessary notifications. Choosing an option does not mean the referenced email feature is already active.
  • Communications: the sender address, message text and anything else you voluntarily email to support, contact, privacy, security, legal or administration. Do not include passwords, access tokens, payment credentials or other unnecessary secrets.

Information generated through use

  • Account and session information: identifiers, email-verification status, login/session details and timestamps necessary for access control and account recovery. Our hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses and device/browser details for abuse prevention and operating logs.
  • Technical events: error and performance information that infrastructure providers may generate while serving pages and API requests. Request routing and security controls may necessarily process network metadata.
  • Limited visitor counts: a first-visit or return-visit event as described below, without deliberately attaching an account identifier, profile text or a page-by-page browsing history.
  • Founder eligibility: if your saved profile is among the first 250 awarded slots, the system stores a founder number, award timestamp, standard lifetime OG Status entitlement, and a future Founder Profile badge marker. "Triple OG" is a nickname, not a multiplier.

Information we do not currently require as site features

We do not currently run member photo uploads, internal person-to-person chat, paid checkout, government-ID verification, face recognition or continuous GPS tracking. This is not a promise that an ordinary web host sees no technical metadata or that a future feature will never require a separate notice.

3. Where information comes from

Most personal data comes directly from you when you register, edit a profile, answer questions, choose settings or email us. Session and technical data also come from your browser and the providers running our website and authentication. Town and city labels and coordinates in the location picker come from the public GeoNames locality dataset, combined with the place you selected; the geographic reference data alone does not identify you.

We do not currently buy third-party dating dossiers, import social-network contact lists, or retrieve private information from other dating services to enrich member accounts.

4. Why we process it

  • Deliver membership features: register and verify accounts, recognize signed-in members, save private profiles and match answers, allow edits and account deletion, and remember chosen settings.
  • Protect users and the service: authenticate requests, help prevent abuse or unauthorized access, investigate credible reports and protect infrastructure.
  • Respond to you: handle support, privacy, security and rights requests, and send verification, account recovery or other necessary messages.
  • Improve what we build: diagnose failures, test feature reliability, review aggregate usage and develop functions such as compatibility matching without turning private profile content into a public index.
  • Follow the law: respond to legally valid requests, protect legal rights, keep records when required and prevent unlawful misuse.

Where local law requires a specific legal basis for processing, the applicable basis may be providing the service you requested, legitimate interests in securing and improving it, compliance with law, or your consent where needed. We do not treat a broad statement in this notice as consent for new sensitive-data uses that legally require a separate choice.

5. Sensitive data and your choices

Dating information may be especially personal. A profile can reveal sexual orientation, relationship preferences, religion, politics, health-related matters or other information you include voluntarily, whether or not the site asks for it. You do not need to provide an exact address or other unnecessary sensitive information to make a basic profile.

During the current limited launch, these entries are saved privately for your account. If future search, messaging, references, awards or public-profile features would share some of that information with other members, the site must explain the new visibility and relevant controls rather than silently treating private content as public.

6. Visibility of profiles, answers and planned features

Currently: member records are private-by-default and an authenticated member profile API is intended to return only the signed-in member's data. There is no current public directory of other members' private accounts or public server-side matching/discovery feed. Question answers marked private remain account data; a future compatibility calculation or sharing feature would need new implementation and notice.

Planned, not live: photo uploads, in-app messages, member-to-member discovery, community profile voting and paid membership. Their final data visibility, controls, retention and reporting rules have not been implemented. This policy will be revised if they launch, with further notice or consent when required. Please do not assume that a private profile today will remain completely invisible after you opt into future discovery.

Separately, material voluntarily published on a public website can be copied or cached by other people, platforms or search engines. We cannot recall their independent copies merely by deleting a record in our own database.

7. Who receives data

We share only as needed to operate the site or for a legitimate legal or safety purpose. Current provider categories include:

  • Render: delivery and hosting of public website files and associated hosting/network operations.
  • Neon: managed sign-in and account verification, backend functions, and private PostgreSQL storage for member information and settings.
  • The domain's email host: delivery, storage and processing of messages you send to our @ogcupid.com addresses.
  • GoatCounter: minimal anonymous-intent visit-event counting described in the next section; it may see standard network information when a count request reaches it.

We may disclose relevant information if reasonably necessary to respond to a legally valid order or request, defend legal rights, investigate security incidents, address serious safety issues, or comply with applicable law. We do not promise never to comply with a lawful subpoena or warrant.

We do not currently sell member profiles or match answers, provide third-party advertisers with a member-data feed, or use third-party advertising pixels to build behavioral ad audiences. We do not use members' dating answers to target third-party ads. A material change to these practices would require a policy update and any rights or choices that law requires.

8. Cookies, browser storage and analytics

Necessary session and login mechanisms may use cookies or other identifiers so you can sign in securely. Some quiz or interface state may be stored locally in your browser. Deleting browser storage may sign you out or reset a local-only quiz, and blocking essential session storage may make sign-in unusable.

On ogcupid.onrender.com, our minimal GoatCounter integration sends only an event name of first-visit or return-visit. It deliberately does not send your account ID, a member profile, search terms, page title or referrer. Local browser keys tell the script whether the browser has previously visited and limit reporting to once within an eight-hour window. Those marker values stay in the browser rather than being transmitted as identities. A count request may still reveal ordinary network metadata, such as an IP address, to the receiving provider.

That counter respects supported browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals and does not interfere with browsing when blocked. These signals are not represented as an all-purpose way to block essential authentication, anti-abuse logs, or other functions the site needs.

9. Service messages, preferences and contact email

We may send necessary messages such as verification codes, password recovery or essential account/security notices. A signed-in member may choose optional categories for site news and certain future account activities. Some notification categories are still planned; selecting them does not guarantee delivery until a feature is implemented.

Messages to support@ogcupid.com, privacy@ogcupid.com, or other listed mailboxes will be handled through the domain's hosted mail service. Avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary to resolve your issue. We may retain and use correspondence to reply, keep appropriate records, resolve disputes or investigate safety incidents.

10. How long information is kept

In general, we keep active account and profile information while an account is maintained or as otherwise necessary to provide the requested service. We keep technical records as needed for security and operations, and contact correspondence as needed to respond, document a request or meet lawful obligations. We have not established a single guaranteed deletion deadline covering every service provider's logs and backups.

When a member completes self-service deletion, the active account and linked application rows are designed to be removed from the production database. An issued founder slot is not made available to another account by that deletion, but the deleted profile's identifiable founder award row is removed with the account. Deleted data may remain temporarily in provider backups, delivered email, security records, or copies previously held by third parties until those separate systems expire or are lawfully cleared.

We may retain limited information where necessary to investigate fraud, prevent repeated abuse, honor legal preservation duties or resolve disputes. If you want to understand or challenge the handling of a particular record, contact privacy@ogcupid.com.

11. Reviewing, editing and deleting your information

When signed in, you can review and update much of your profile, answers and available preferences. Account deletion, where available, requires confirmation of your current credentials and removes linked data from the active application database as described above.

By writing to privacy@ogcupid.com, you may ask what information we have about you, request a correction, request deletion, ask for an accessible copy, object to or restrict certain uses, or ask questions about a prior request. Whether any particular statutory right applies depends on your jurisdiction and the circumstances. We may ask for sufficient information to verify that a person requesting account records is entitled to receive them.

We will address applicable legal rights within the relevant legal timelines. Where a legal right to appeal a privacy request decision or appoint an authorized agent applies, tell us and provide the information required to evaluate it. Please do not email your password or verification code to establish your identity.

12. U.S. state privacy rights and sales of data

Residents of some U.S. states may have legal rights to access, correct, erase, obtain or restrict processing of certain personal information; to opt out of covered sales, targeted advertising or certain automated profiling; and, in some circumstances, to appeal a denied request. The details and business-coverage thresholds differ by state. We will evaluate requests under laws applicable to OG Cupid rather than claim every state's requirements automatically apply to this project.

As currently configured, OG Cupid does not sell personal information or share member profiles for cross-context behavioral advertising. Accordingly, we do not currently offer a separate paid-data-sale opt-out workflow. You can still email privacy@ogcupid.com with an opt-out inquiry or an applicable rights request.

13. Visitors outside the United States

Our site uses U.S.-oriented hosting, authentication and processing providers. Information may be stored or processed in the United States or other locations where providers operate. Privacy rules and rights vary across countries. OG Cupid has not claimed special certification, a local representative, or compliance with every jurisdiction's dating-site requirements.

If the privacy law where you live applies to the Service, you may have additional rights regarding access, correction, erasure, portability, objection, restriction, consent, or complaints to a supervisory authority. Write to privacy@ogcupid.com with an applicable request. We will not represent that a region-specific transfer mechanism or legal representative is already in place unless it has actually been established.

14. Matching, automated processing and AI

Question randomization, compatibility calculations and personality-related features may use software rules. Current quizzes and saved match questions are entertainment and profile-building tools; the site does not presently use an automated system to make legal decisions about users or assign eligibility for credit, employment, insurance or housing. Founder numbering is determined by a chronological, database-backed allocation rule for saved profiles, not by a machine-learning model evaluating a person's worth.

Future matching and community-award features may use scores or rankings. We will describe any material automated evaluation or visibility choices before launch and provide additional choices where applicable law requires them. Publication of an article assisted by AI is not evidence that private member data is being submitted to an external AI training service.

15. Keeping account information secure

OG Cupid uses HTTPS for its public deployment, managed authentication, signed-session checks and database permissions intended to limit member access to their own private records. We restrict private member records from the static public website repository. Infrastructure providers may implement additional protective measures under their own security programs.

No online system is completely immune to mistakes, outages, attacks or data loss. Protect your credentials, use a unique password, and contact security@ogcupid.com promptly if you suspect an incident. Do not email passwords, tokens or unredacted government IDs. We will provide incident notice when legally required.

16. Children and adult-only membership

OG Cupid is intended only for adults aged 18 or older, and children may not register. We do not intentionally provide accounts to children or knowingly collect their information as members. If you believe a child has registered or submitted personal information, please contact privacy@ogcupid.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
