Privacy Policy

Here's what the OG Cupid site currently collects, why, and what choices you have while we build.

Effective and last updated: November 12, 2026

Information collectedHow it is usedProviders & sharingYour choicesRetentionSecurity

OG Cupid is an independently operated dating-site project. This notice explains the personal information the website currently handles and how to contact us. The site is still evolving, so this policy will be updated as new features actually launch.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and verification: email address, display name, authentication and verification information, password credentials handled through our managed authentication provider, and account/session metadata needed for login and recovery. The member-profile database does not store plaintext passwords.
  • Profile information you choose to provide: your age, approximate city or town, gender and relationship preferences, biography and essays, lifestyle details, and other fields in the profile editor. These can include sensitive details if you enter them; please share thoughtfully.
  • Match-question answers and preferences: your answers, answers you find acceptable, importance ratings, optional notes and privacy choices. Location search preferences may include a chosen town and approximate town-center coordinates, not your home address or browser GPS.
  • Email preferences: your choices about optional account and site notifications. Choosing a category does not mean the corresponding email feature has already been launched.
  • Messages you email us: your email address and whatever you include in your correspondence to our contact, support, privacy, security, legal or admin mailboxes. Don't send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication services may receive basic network and device information (such as IP address, browser details, timestamps, and diagnostic/security logs) needed to operate and defend the service.

2. Why we use it

We use account information to create and verify memberships, authenticate sessions, provide support, recover access, handle deletion requests and protect the service. Profile and match-question information is used to save your work and support the site's planned matching features. We also use technical information to maintain and troubleshoot the website, prevent abuse and understand basic traffic.

3. Profile visibility and features still in development

At this stage, member profiles and answers are stored privately and are not available through a public member-search or discovery service. In-app member messaging, member photo uploads, and payment processing are not currently operating. If these features launch, we'll update this policy and explain the relevant visibility and data uses before enabling them. Do not assume that a future public dating profile will always remain private.

4. Service providers and disclosure

OG Cupid currently uses Render for website hosting and Neon for managed authentication, backend services and PostgreSQL storage. These providers process data necessary to operate the website under their own terms and infrastructure practices. Domain-hosted email services process messages you send to our published addresses. We may disclose information when necessary to respond to lawful requests, defend legal rights, or address serious safety or security concerns.

We do not currently sell member-profile data, operate a behavioral advertising marketplace, or give other dating members access to your private profile through a discovery API.

5. Cookies, local storage, and visitor counts

Authentication uses session-related browser and server mechanisms. The site may also use browser storage for local interface state, such as an unfinished match question. On the hosted site, a minimal GoatCounter visitor counter sends one of two fixed event names—first visit or return visit—at most once per eight-hour window. It does not deliberately send your profile, account ID, page title, search query or referring page. A local browser marker determines which event to count; the analytics provider and hosting infrastructure may still receive ordinary network information such as an IP address as part of receiving a request. The counter honors supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals and can fail without affecting the site.

6. Your choices and requests

  • You can review and change your saved profile and email preferences when signed in.
  • Eligible signed-in members can use the account deletion controls, which require a current-password confirmation, to remove the account and connected application records from the active database.
  • You can contact privacy@ogcupid.com about privacy questions, access, corrections or deletion help. We may need to verify your identity before acting on an account-specific request.
  • You can adjust browser storage and privacy preferences, but disabling essential session mechanisms may prevent login.

7. Retention and deletion

We generally retain accounts and associated profiles while they remain active, subject to necessary operational, security and legal needs. Deleting an account removes related application records from the active database; it does not instantly erase provider backups, already-delivered emails, security logs, or independently retained copies. The retention windows for backups and logs depend on the applicable provider settings and legal requirements; we cannot promise that every copy disappears immediately.

Contact emails are retained as needed to respond and maintain appropriate records, subject to legal and security obligations. If you need help removing or correcting information, write to privacy@ogcupid.com.

8. Security and limitations

We use managed authentication, verified sessions and private database access controls to protect member records. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Please use a strong, unique password and report suspected vulnerabilities or account access issues to security@ogcupid.com. Do not send credentials in email.

9. Adults only

OG Cupid is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Minors may not create accounts. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact privacy@ogcupid.com so we can investigate.

10. International visitors and updates

OG Cupid currently operates through U.S.-based service infrastructure and is not specifically configured for every country's privacy requirements. If you access the site from elsewhere, your information may be processed in the United States or wherever our providers operate. Applicable privacy rights vary by jurisdiction; contact us to exercise rights you believe apply to you. Material changes to our data practices will be reflected in an updated notice, with additional notice where required.
