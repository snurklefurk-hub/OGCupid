# OG Cupid — Product Vision (Draft)

_Started October 31, 2026. This is a planning document, not an implementation specification._

## One-line pitch

**Come for the quizzes. Stay for the people.**

## Why this exists

Modern swipe-style dating apps often make profiles feel interchangeable and reduce discovery to photos and engagement loops. OG Cupid aims to bring back the sense of discovering genuinely interesting people through rich profiles, essays, compatibility questions, journals, and community-created content.

## Core commitments

### Rich, required member profiles
- No empty or perfunctory profiles shown to other members.
- Participation in member discovery/messaging requires a substantive profile, regardless of membership payment.
- Support written essays, personality and interest sections, intentions, and personal details with member privacy controls.
- Define fair and accessible minimum standards **later**; do not confuse word count with character or quality.

### Compatibility
- Members answer reusable compatibility questions.
- Per question, members select their answer, what partner answers they accept, and how important the question is.
- Explain compatibility percentages and the limitations of scores; do not imply precise scientific accuracy.
- Offer search/filtering beyond shallow photo-based swiping.

### User-generated quizzes and tests
- Members create, take, publish and explore quizzes.
- Results can be displayed voluntarily on personal profiles.
- A public quiz catalog may help people discover OG Cupid before subscribing.
- Include moderation and content standards to prevent harassment and abuse.

### Journals and community
- Personal journals/blog-style posts are a central part of the experience.
- Discussion areas and social discovery support friendships and interesting conversations in addition to dating.
- Keep content browsing organized and searchable.

### Member activity transparency
- An honest **Last online X days ago** indicator appears on discoverable profiles.
- Provide last-active date range filters in member search.
- Approximate time buckets are preferable to precise timestamps; protect users at risk of stalking.
- No fake 'online now,' recycled activity, or promoted zombie profiles.
- Explore a separate 'profile last updated' indicator, but it is not yet committed.

### Membership and OG Status
- Initial proposal: everybody pays **$2.99 for the first month**.
- A recurring monthly fee for non-OG members is being considered; **final price undecided**.
- Members can earn **permanent OG Status as early as day one** by building an outstanding profile and contributing meaningfully to the community.
- OG Status permanently waives future monthly membership fees.
- Community participation may be evaluated monthly or every two months, but **never as required daily/weekly streaks**.
- Inactivity alone does not cancel earned OG Status.
- **Detailed qualification criteria deferred** for later design; avoid automated judgments of social desirability, invasive monitoring of messages, popularity rankings, or pressure to respond.

### Platform
- Website-only launch; desktop-focused but usable and accessible on mobile browsers.
- Native apps are explicitly out of scope initially.
- Consider a web-wrapper app only after traction, with app-store compliance and a reason to exist beyond an icon.

## Visual and experiential direction

- Early-to-late-2000s web personality: meaningful navigation, roomy desktop pages, profile individuality, fun quizzes and badges, journals, community spaces.
- **Not** a generic modern swipe application with retro colors pasted on.
- Modern requirements: responsive layout, accessibility, account security, data safety, moderation tools, usable onboarding, and clear privacy settings.

## Explicitly unresolved

- Actual brand name and trademark clearance.
- Tech stack, hosting, architecture, database.
- Profile completion/quality criteria and inclusive accessibility rules.
- OG Status earning criteria and review/appeal process.
- Actual post-first-month subscription price and cancellation/refund logic.
- How granular last-seen activity appears, and safety exceptions.
- Community structure and moderation policies.
- Exact beta features, launch market, and rollout timeline.

## Design guardrails

- No payment-for-ranking or artificial scarcity.
- No streak incentives that force compulsory social interaction.
- No pretending dormant accounts are online.
- No copying another service's source code, protected assets, or personal data.
- No launch without safety, privacy, abuse reporting and age-appropriate access rules.
