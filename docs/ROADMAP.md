# OG Cupid — Working Roadmap

This is a **discussion-first outline**, not a commitment to ship on particular dates.

## Phase 0 — Rediscover the good stuff
- Gather and review historical early-OkCupid screenshots, user journeys and features as design references.
- Separate historical inspiration from proprietary branding/content.
- Define the old-web visual direction without sacrificing modern usability.
- Confirm trademark-safe product name before public marketing.

## Phase 1 — Product foundations
- Agree on the MVP scope and technical stack.
- Specify profile fields, completion gate and account settings.
- Design compatibility question schema, scoring approach and explainability.
- Decide how 'last online' is calculated and shown safely.
- Define membership, $2.99 entry payment, recurring billing and earned permanent OG status.
- Write moderation, consent, age restriction, reporting and privacy requirements.

## Phase 2 — Website MVP (initial target)
- Sign up, age gate, secure authentication and account management.
- Substantive profile creation, editing and controlled visibility.
- Search and browse, including last-active filters.
- Compatibility question answering and meaningful match indicators.
- Private messaging with block/report tools and anti-spam controls.
- Journals and a basic quiz library/creator.
- Payment integration and an auditable OG status entitlement.
- Mobile-browser responsiveness and desktop-first navigation.

## Phase 3 — Community beta
- Invite limited members and test whether profiles are actually thoughtful and interactions healthy.
- Tune quiz discovery, journals, search and community discussions.
- Audit moderation capacity and prevent spam, impersonation and harassment.
- Measure meaningful participation (without public scoreboards or inbox-count contests).

## Later, only with traction
- Richer forums and community spaces.
- More personality-test types and community-curated quiz discovery.
- Possible thin web-wrapper apps, subject to app-store rules.
- Expansion of earned OG pathways after the criteria are agreed.

## Deliberately not yet specified
- OG Status qualification thresholds: **revisit later**.
- Technology choices: select after working through product/user flows.
- Feature release dates: do not promise them before scope and effort are known.
