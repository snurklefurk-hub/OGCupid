# Wayback discovery — 2026-11-02

The alternate Internet Archive availability API located two original Dating Persona Test landing pages. Following their actual links recovered all 32 result pages, each with a result description and both Always avoid and Consider sections. Captures resolve to January 17–February 5, 2008, as verified from the playback metadata in each returned page.

## What was recovered

- Two test landing-page captures: January 7 and February 22, 2008.
- Source links and availability checks for all 32 result pages.
- Missing-result sources for The Bachelor, The 5-Night Stand, and The Stiletto.
- Complete source pages for the partial Manchild and Poolboy descriptions.
- The Gentleman's previously missing avoid guidance points to The Battleaxe.
- Two original stylesheets, with 55 linked image references in total. These include test navigation buttons, page backgrounds, panels, and signup/login graphics.
- Original form metadata: a POST to online.dating.persona.test, internal test name oktest3, starting with page 1 and next-page 2.

## What remains unverified

The CDX directory inventory returned a 503 response; a smaller prefix query timed out. This pass therefore used snapshot availability lookup and links extracted from actual archived pages, rather than a directory-wide inventory.

The landing pages contain no questionnaire items and no recovered scoring weights. They submit a POST form to begin the test. This is evidence about the original entry flow, not proof that the full questionnaire or scoring engine has survived.

Two additional linked assets, the selected super.js and main1.css versions, returned 404. The two successfully retrieved stylesheets are recorded in the companion JSON, along with their referenced asset URLs.

The live website was not changed during this research pass. The inventory records source locations and factual recommendation lists, not a redistributed copy of the complete original prose.
