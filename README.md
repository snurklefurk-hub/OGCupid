# OG Cupid

**Come for the quizzes. Stay for the people.**

OG Cupid is a web-first community and dating platform inspired by the personality-rich internet of the 2000s: expressive profiles, compatibility questions, user-created quizzes, personal journals, and genuine discovery instead of endless swiping.

> **Project status:** Early planning. No production application exists yet. Product decisions below are preliminary unless marked as core commitments.

## What we're bringing back

- **Substantive profiles, not placeholders.** Members complete meaningful profiles before participating in member discovery or messaging. Paid members do not bypass this requirement.
- **Compatibility that means something.** Members answer questions, select which answers they would accept from matches, and weight how much each question matters.
- **User-created quizzes and personality tests.** Playful, serious, niche, and delightfully weird quizzes; share results on profiles.
- **Personal journals.** People can be discovered through what they write, not just their photos.
- **Community beyond dating.** Explore discussions, find friends, and meet interesting people without pressure to pursue romance.
- **Browse instead of swipe.** Search and discover members by interests, compatibility, and other explicit preferences.
- **Honest activity indicators.** Show an approximate "last online" timestamp on discoverable profiles, with last-active search filters. Do not make inactive accounts look active.
- **Old-web soul, modern foundations.** A desktop-first site that also works well in mobile browsers, with modern security, accessibility, and privacy protections.

## Membership direction (proposed)

The tentative model is a **$2.99 first-month entry price** and a modest recurring membership for members who have not earned OG Status. Final pricing and billing details remain undecided.

**OG Status:** A member can qualify *on their very first day* by creating an outstanding profile **and** genuinely contributing to the community. Once earned, OG Status permanently waives recurring membership charges. It must not require waiting a minimum number of days or buying additional access.

The detailed criteria for OG Status will be designed later. Broad participation can be reviewed on a monthly or roughly bimonthly cadence, **not** as a daily/weekly activity treadmill. Ordinary inactivity must not revoke permanently earned OG Status.

Our goal is to reward contributions, not message volume, popularity, compulsory replies, or private conversations analyzed by AI. No visibility boosts or better compatibility scores sold to paying members.

## The experience

**A proper website first.** Desktop is a first-class experience, and mobile web must remain usable. No native app is planned for launch. A web-wrapper app might be considered later if it adds meaningful value and meets app-store rules.

## Project principles

1. **People over engagement metrics.** No artificial scarcity, dark patterns, or swipe casino.
2. **Personality over presentation.** Interesting people should be discoverable for their words and ideas.
3. **Truthful signals.** Show real account inactivity; never fabricate engagement.
4. **Free forever should be earnable.** Exceptional contributors can waive recurring fees immediately.
5. **Safety is a requirement.** The platform needs age gating, strong account and abuse defenses, reporting/blocking, moderation, and thoughtful privacy controls before public launch.
6. **Build independently.** This is an original implementation, not a copy of any other site's proprietary code, branding, or user data.
