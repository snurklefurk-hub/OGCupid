How were my scores calculated?

Seven original statements measure each of four dimensions. Answers are weighted -2, -1, +1 or +2, with reverse-worded statements counted in the opposite direction. Positive scores map to the first letter of each pair; negative scores map to the second. On a zero score, the majority direction breaks the tie. The result is one of 16 codes, each with two selectable classic character names. This is an entertainment quiz and its scoring has not been validated scientifically.
